Support the project, get alerts

Orlando Call Map is independent and free to use. Supporters choose a monthly amount and get a text or email when police, fire, rescue or a crash response is dispatched near an address they pick, and browse the site without ads. Covers calls inside Orlando city limits.

Monthly support
Minimum $3 a month. Cancel anytime. Support payments go to running the site and sending alerts. They aren't charitable donations and aren't tax-deductible.
Alert me about

Alerts come from the departments' dispatch logs. Early reports can be vague or wrong, sensitive calls such as domestic violence are withheld, and only calls inside Orlando city limits are included.
