Privacy Policy

Have a lawyer review this page before launch. It is a starting draft.

What we collect

If you subscribe: your email, optional mobile number, the addresses and radii you choose, and your alert preferences. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.

How we use it

Only to send the alerts you asked for and messages about your subscription. We don't sell or share your information, except with the providers that deliver the service (Stripe for billing, our email and SMS providers for delivery, and address-lookup services to place your address on the map).

Your choices

Change or delete your alert areas anytime from your manage link. Cancel your subscription and email us to have your data deleted. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.
